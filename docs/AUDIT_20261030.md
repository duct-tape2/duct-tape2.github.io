# Site audit: SEO, conversion, quality (2026-10-30)

Scope: every file served from this repository: 25 HTML pages (10 content pages including `404.html`, 15 redirect stubs that forward to `/repo-launch/`), `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `feed.xml`, `llms.txt`, `offers.json`, `README.md`, `PUBLIC_ENGINEERING_PROFILE.md` and the Markdown downloads.

Method:
- `scripts/check_site.py`, added in this audit, for links, anchors, meta tags, JSON-LD and the sitemap.
- A headless Chromium pass at 375×800 for horizontal overflow, text contrast (WCAG AA), accessible names and heading order, with before/after screenshots.
- Manual reading of every content page.

Live URLs on `duct-tape2.github.io` and other `duct-tape2/*` repositories could not be fetched from the audit sandbox. Same-host project pages from other repositories are therefore listed as "not checked".

Severity: **High** = breaks a buyer path or an SEO signal. **Medium** = weakens a signal or accessibility. **Low** = polish.

## Needs the owner's decision

1. **Korean content.** The site has no Korean text. Every page is `lang="en"`, and `offers.json`, JSON-LD and copy say the service is available in English only. Adding Korean pages changes the service promise (language), so this audit did not add them. If you want Korean, decide on: a `/ko/` mirror of `/` and `/repo-launch/`, `hreflang="ko"` alternates, and `available_language` in `offers.json`.
2. **Live starter URL.** Links now point to `https://duct-tape2.github.io/github-pages-storefront-starter/`, assumed to be served by the `github-pages-storefront-starter` repo. Please confirm it loads; it could not be fetched from the audit sandbox. The same applies to `/ai-language-partner/` and `/japanese-anki-pack/`.
3. **Delivery-start wording differs between pages.** The pages variously say "after confirmation", "after scope confirmation", "after scope and assets are confirmed", and "after scope, assets, and a working payment path are confirmed". `offers.json` says "after the public repo, scope, assets, and payment are confirmed". The wording was left as written. Pick one wording and it can be applied everywhere.
4. **Google Fonts on `/work/` and `/design/`.** Removing them makes the pages faster and avoids a third-party request, but changes the typography to system fonts. Self-hosting the fonts is the middle option.
5. **Brand entity type.** JSON-LD describes the provider as an `Organization` on sales pages and as a `Person` on `/work/`. Both are kept with stable `@id`s. Choose one if you want a single knowledge-graph entity.
6. **Retired products.** `free-checklist.md` still advertises a "Paid starter kit", and `.github/FUNDING.yml` links `/buy/`. Update or remove them.
7. **Public email address.** `sks7178@gmail.com` appears in plain text and in JSON-LD on many pages, which invites spam. Keep it, or rely on the issue form?
8. **`/favicon.ico`.** Browsers that ignore SVG favicons will still request `/favicon.ico` and get a 404. Add a `.ico` if that matters to you.
